### 🏠 Dataset Features Explained

| Feature        | Meaning                                                                 |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **MedInc**     | Median income of households in the area (in $10,000s)                  |
| **HouseAge**   | Age of the houses in the area (in years)                               |
| **AveRooms**   | Average number of rooms per household                                   |
| **AveOccup**   | Average number of people living per household                          |
| **Latitude**   | Geographic latitude of the location (north–south position)             |
| **Longitude**  | Geographic longitude of the location (east–west position)              |
| **MedHouseVal**| Median house value in the area (in $100,000s) — this is your target 🎯 |
